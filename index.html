Quick ID guide

Spot a fish? Find what it looks like, then follow the clue.

Big & orange

Huge, long tail fin → Thatcher

Smaller, weaker one → Ethan

Blue with a red eye

No stripe on its side → Connor

Dark stripe across its side → Rohan

Tiny orange trio

Biggest → Gabe

Middle size → Amit

Smallest & skinniest → Caden

Spotted, stuck to stuff

The algae-eater clinging to wood or glass → Stella

Giant flowing fins

Blue half moon betta → F.R.O.G. T.W.O.

Not a fish at all

The two little frogs → Jessica & Liv

Meet the crew

Goldfish

Thatcher & Ethan

  • Thatcher has a much bigger rear fin. That long, flowing tail is the giveaway.
  • Ethan is the smaller, weaker of the two.
  • Ethan can sometimes be caught eating his own poop.

Blue & red-eyed

Connor

  • No stripe on his sides. That's how you know it's Connor and not Rohan.

Blue & red-eyed

Rohan

  • Has a dark stripe running across his sides.
  • Looks a lot like Connor, so check for the stripe!

The trio

Caden, Amit & Gabe

These three look alike, so go by size:

Cadensmallest & skinniest
Amitthe Goldilocks: medium build
Gabelargest of the three

Pleco

Stella

  • The only pleco in the tank: spotted and usually suctioned onto the driftwood or glass.
  • She's the cleanup crew. Look under the wood if you can't find her.

Half moon betta

F.R.O.G. T.W.O.

  • Impossible to miss: big, flowing blue fins that fan out like a half moon.
  • A little quirky. He does his own thing.
  • Yes, he's a fish. No, we won't explain the name.

African dwarf frog

Jessica

  • One of the tank's two gold African dwarf frogs. The other one is Liv.
  • Often spotted paddling up and down along the glass.

African dwarf frog

Liv

  • One of the tank's two gold African dwarf frogs. The other one is Jessica.
  • Watch for her webbed back feet spread wide as she paddles up for air.
